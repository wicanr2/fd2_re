ID1 compositor correction and exact data (2026-08-23)
------------------------------------------------------

The earlier compact ID1 sentence above incorrectly attributed a direct sample
marker to 0x262EF. Fresh IDA instruction review proves that this entry calls
neither 0x25A96 nor 0x25B45. Audio belongs to the outer 0x2B659/caller family;
the editable ID1 schedule therefore has no local sample marker.

- 0x52420 signed int32 X values: `-59,-39,0,39,55,39,0,-39`.
- 0x52440 signed int32 Y values: `-10,-24,-30,-24,-10,4,10,4`.
- mode3 counters: `0,-2,-4,-6,-8,-10,-12,-14`.
- mode4 draws slots0..3 as frame=counter at offsets0..3, then slots4..7
  as frame=counter+15 at offsets0..3.
- mode5 draws slots0..3 as frame=counter+15 at offsets4..7, then slots4..7
  as frame=counter at offsets4..7; it increments all counters and returns
  complete when any incremented counter equals9. Because slot0 starts at0,
  the target loop therefore presents nine mode4→target→mode5 frames.
- Both modes draw only counters in `[0,15)`. Destination includes X anchor
  `+0x50`; raw actor `+6==0` adds another `+0x94` to X.
- 0x2935B logical ABI is `(effectResource,frame,destination,stride,-1)`.
- 0x2A6BD selects FDOTHER #19 for raw actor `+6!=0` and #21 for `+6==0`.
  Both are 30-frame FIGANI resources; respective resource MD5 values are
  `7d217fc16e58e4fbd64ae0a9ddfff211` and
  `8b6d7f2e01f3246033c322c3ab6233be`.

This closes the pure indexed compositor as `DATA-READY`; MP/HP/audio/action
publication order and a normal unmodified enemy-turn capture remain outside
0x262EF, so production transaction wiring remains fail-closed.
