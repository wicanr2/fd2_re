ID1 compositor correction and exact data (2026-08-23)
------------------------------------------------------

The earlier compact ID1 sentence above incorrectly attributed a direct sample
marker to 0x262EF. Fresh IDA instruction review proves that this entry calls
neither 0x25A96 nor 0x25B45. Audio belongs to the outer 0x2B659/caller family;
the editable ID1 schedule therefore has no local sample marker.

- 0x52420 signed int32 X values: `-59,-39,0,39,55,39,0,-39`.
- 0x52440 signed int32 Y values: `-10,-24,-30,-24,-10,4,10,4`.
- mode3 counters: `0,-2,-4,-6,-8,-10,-12,-14`.
- mode4 draws slots0..3 as frame=counter at offsets0..3, then slots4..7
  as frame=counter+15 at offsets0..3.
- mode5 draws slots0..3 as frame=counter+15 at offsets4..7, then slots4..7
  as frame=counter at offsets4..7; it increments all counters and returns
  complete when any incremented counter equals9. Because slot0 starts at0,
  the target loop therefore presents nine mode4→target→mode5 frames.
- Both modes draw only counters in `[0,15)`. Destination includes X anchor
  `+0x50`; raw actor `+6==0` adds another `+0x94` to X.
- 0x2935B logical ABI is `(effectResource,frame,destination,stride,-1)`.
- 0x2A6BD selects FDOTHER #19 for raw actor `+6!=0` and #21 for `+6==0`.
  Both are 30-frame FIGANI resources; respective resource MD5 values are
  `7d217fc16e58e4fbd64ae0a9ddfff211` and
  `8b6d7f2e01f3246033c322c3ab6233be`.

This closes the pure indexed compositor as `DATA-READY`; MP/HP/audio/action
publication order and a normal unmodified enemy-turn capture remain outside

ID1 outer-loop correction (2026-08-24)
--------------------------------------

Fresh IDA Pro 9.4 analysis initially exposed a database-boundary hazard: IDA
merged 0x262EF into the preceding `sub_26152`, and Hex-Rays consequently
produced a contradictory seven-slot body. Direct instructions from the fixed
FD2.EXE fingerprint, independently decoded with Capstone 5.0.3, resolve the
actual contract without renaming either original function:

- `0x2630C/0x2631A` copy eight X and eight Y dwords; `0x26374..0x26379`
  initializes eight counters and returns **31**, not nine.
- mode4 draws slots0..3 at `0x26391..0x263D4`, then slots4..7 at
  `0x263D6..0x26433`; mode5 draws the swapped groups at
  `0x26448..0x264E1`. The earlier eight-slot layer description remains valid.
- `0x264E3..0x26526` increments all eight counters on every mode5 call. It
  returns numeric marker1 whenever a counter becomes9, producing eight HP
  markers at target-frame steps `8,10,12,14,16,18,20,22` across the full
  31-frame target budget. These align exactly with command1's denominator8.
- The same increment loop directly calls `0x25A96(sound,1,1)` whenever a
  counter becomes5, producing eight local sample markers at steps
  `4,6,8,10,12,14,16,18`. Therefore the older claim that `0x262EF` has no
  direct sample call is withdrawn; it resulted from an incomplete function

- Steps23..30 remain part of the owner loop even after the last numeric marker;
  they may present only the target idle layer after all effect counters leave
